TiendaNube Connector for Odoo

The complete bridge between your Odoo backend and your TiendaNube store

External service required

This app integrates Odoo with the TiendaNube (Nuvemshop) API. A TiendaNube store and a partner application (Client ID / Client Secret) are required. After you authorize the app with OAuth, products, stock and orders are exchanged between your Odoo instance and TiendaNube servers.

Everything you need to run your store from Odoo

Products & Stock

  • Create publications from Odoo products and export them to TiendaNube
  • Import existing TiendaNube publications into Odoo
  • Bring images, variants and attributes with each publication
  • Push stock from Odoo to TiendaNube, on demand or automatically when you enable it
  • Pause and republish listings when stock crosses the thresholds you set

Orders & Customers

  • Import TiendaNube orders and create Odoo sales orders
  • Webhooks import orders as they arrive; a poll every 10 minutes catches what the webhook missed
  • Match publications and order lines to Odoo products by SKU
  • Optionally create and post the customer invoice when an order is imported
  • Connect the store with OAuth 2.0; tokens stay in your Odoo database

Up and running in minutes

  1. Install the module and open TiendaNube → Configuration.
  2. Enter the Client ID and Client Secret from your TiendaNube partner app on that configuration. Saving copies them into the system parameters; you do not enter them there by hand.
  3. Click Authorize Application and sign in on TiendaNube. Store ID and tokens are filled in automatically. Then use Test Connection.
  4. Choose the warehouse and stock type, and set the customer contact used on sales orders and invoices.
  5. Import existing publications, or create them from Odoo products and export them.
  6. Turn on automatic stock sync, minimum-stock pause and automatic invoicing if you want them. Order webhooks and polling are already on.

Data & privacy

Once authorized, the connector exchanges only the data required for e-commerce operations — products, variants, stock levels, orders and the customer and shipping details provided by TiendaNube. All credentials and tokens are stored exclusively in your own Odoo database. No activation key, no third-party server, no lock-in. You own your data at all times.
