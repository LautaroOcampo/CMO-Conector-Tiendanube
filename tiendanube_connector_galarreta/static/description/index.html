TiendaNube Connector for Odoo

The complete bridge between your Odoo backend and your TiendaNube store

External service required

This app integrates Odoo with the TiendaNube (Nuvemshop) API. A TiendaNube store and a partner application (Client ID / Client Secret) are required. After a one-click OAuth authorization, all product, stock and order data flows securely between your Odoo instance and TiendaNube servers.

Everything you need to run your store from Odoo

📦 Products & Stock

  • Publish Odoo products to TiendaNube in one click
  • Import existing TiendaNube publications into Odoo
  • Sync images, variants and attributes automatically
  • Keep stock levels accurate across both platforms
  • Auto-pause publications when stock runs out — and reactivate when it's back

🛒 Orders & Customers

  • Import TiendaNube orders into Odoo as sales orders in real time
  • Customer records created automatically from order data
  • Match TiendaNube products to Odoo products by SKU
  • Secure one-click connection via OAuth 2.0
  • Scheduled polling as backup for guaranteed order capture

Up and running in minutes

  1. Install the module and open TiendaNube Configuration in Odoo.
  2. Enter the Client ID and Client Secret from your TiendaNube partner app.
  3. Click Authorize to connect your store — tokens are managed automatically.
  4. Import your existing publications or create new ones directly from Odoo.
  5. Enable real-time order import and stock sync — and you're done.

Data & privacy

Once authorized, the connector exchanges only the data required for e-commerce operations — products, variants, stock levels, orders and the customer and shipping details provided by TiendaNube. All credentials and tokens are stored exclusively in your own Odoo database. No activation key, no third-party server, no lock-in. You own your data at all times.
