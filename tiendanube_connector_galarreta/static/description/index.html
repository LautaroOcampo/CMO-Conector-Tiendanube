TiendaNube Connector for Odoo

The complete bridge between Odoo and your TiendaNube store

✅ Odoo 19 Ready 🇦🇷 Built for Latin America ⚡ Real-time webhooks

External service required

This module integrates Odoo with the TiendaNube (Nuvemshop) API. An active TiendaNube store and a partner application (Client ID / Client Secret) are required. Authorization is handled via OAuth 2.0 — your credentials are stored only in your own Odoo database.

Why are TiendaNube & Odoo
better together — if they are connected?

🏪

Manage your store from Odoo

Publish products, update prices and control stock for your TiendaNube store without leaving Odoo. One source of truth for your entire operation.

📦

Stock always in sync

Every sale, delivery or inventory adjustment in Odoo updates your TiendaNube listings automatically. Never oversell again.

🛒

Orders flow automatically

TiendaNube orders arrive in Odoo in real time via webhooks. Customer records, delivery orders and invoices are created automatically.

How it works

1
Install and open TiendaNube Configuration in Odoo

A new TiendaNube menu appears in the Sales app.

2
Enter your Client ID and Client Secret

From your TiendaNube partner app at developers.tiendanube.com.

3
Click Authorize — one click, done

OAuth 2.0 handles the login flow. Tokens are refreshed automatically.

4
Import your publications and link them to Odoo products

Auto-link by SKU or assign manually. Supports variants and attributes.

5
Enable real-time order sync and you're live

Webhooks capture every new order the moment it's placed. Scheduled polling works as a backup.

What gets synchronized

Everything you need to run your store — nothing you don't.

Data & privacy

Once authorized, the connector exchanges only the data required for e-commerce operations — products, variants, stock levels, orders, and the customer and shipping details provided by TiendaNube. All credentials and tokens are stored exclusively in your own Odoo database. No activation key. No third-party server. No lock-in.
